Glow Haven Med Spa – Frequently Asked Questions

What services do you offer?
We offer Botox, dermal fillers, HydraFacials, chemical peels, laser hair removal, microneedling, and IV vitamin therapy. All treatments are performed by licensed nurse injectors or medical aestheticians under the supervision of our medical director, Dr. Sarah Lin.

How much does Botox cost?
Botox is $13 per unit. Most first-time patients need between 20 and 40 units for the forehead and frown lines, so a typical treatment costs $260 to $520. Results appear in 3 to 7 days and last about 3 to 4 months.

How much do fillers cost?
Dermal fillers start at $650 per syringe. Lip filler usually needs one syringe. Cheek and jawline treatments often need two or more. Results last 6 to 18 months depending on the area and product.

Do you offer free consultations?
Yes. Every new client gets a free 20-minute consultation with a nurse injector. We assess your skin, discuss your goals, and give you an exact price before any treatment. There is no obligation to book.

What is a HydraFacial?
A HydraFacial is a 45-minute treatment that cleanses, exfoliates, and hydrates the skin using a gentle suction device. It has no downtime, so you can return to work the same day. A single HydraFacial costs $199, or $549 for a package of three.

How does laser hair removal work?
The laser targets the pigment in hair follicles and stops future growth. Most clients need 6 to 8 sessions, spaced 4 to 6 weeks apart. Prices start at $99 per session for small areas like the upper lip and $299 for full legs.

Is there any downtime after microneedling?
Expect redness similar to a mild sunburn for 24 to 48 hours. Avoid makeup for 24 hours and avoid direct sun for one week. Most clients see smoother skin after 2 to 3 sessions.

What are your opening hours?
We are open Monday to Friday from 10 AM to 7 PM, and Saturday from 9 AM to 4 PM. We are closed on Sundays and public holidays.

Where are you located and is there parking?
We are at 482 Ocean Avenue, Suite 210, Santa Monica, California. Free parking is available in the building garage for up to 2 hours. Validate your ticket at the front desk.

What is your cancellation policy?
Please give at least 24 hours notice to cancel or reschedule. Late cancellations or no-shows are charged a $50 fee, which can be applied toward your next treatment.

Do you offer payment plans?
Yes. We accept all major credit cards, Apple Pay, and financing through Cherry, which lets you split any treatment over 3, 6, or 12 months. Many clients qualify for 0% interest.

Is Botox safe?
Botox is FDA-approved and has been used safely for over 20 years. Common side effects are mild bruising or a small headache that goes away within a day. We do not treat clients who are pregnant or breastfeeding.

Do you have a membership program?
Our Glow Club membership is $99 per month. It includes one HydraFacial or chemical peel every month, 10% off all injectables, and free birthday Botox of up to 10 units.
